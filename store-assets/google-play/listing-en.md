# Google Play Store Listing (English)

## App name

say one thing

## Short description

Send a 30-second voice and start a simple one-to-one conversation.

## Full description

Sometimes you only need to say one thing.

say one thing is a voice-first way to begin a conversation. Record a message of up to 30 seconds and send it to a recently active listener. When they reply, continue the conversation one voice at a time.

HOW IT WORKS

• Sign in with Google or Apple
• Record one short voice message
• Get matched with a recently active listener
• Reply when it is your turn
• Keep your conversations organized in Rooms
• Delete a conversation whenever you want

BUILT FOR VOICE

There is no long post to write and no public feed to manage. Speak naturally, listen when you are ready, and reply with your own voice.

YOUR CHOICE

You can delete individual conversations from the app. You can also delete your account and associated data from the app or from our web account deletion page.

Microphone permission is used to record voice messages. Notifications may be used to let you know when a reply arrives.
